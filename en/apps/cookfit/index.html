How do you find recipes from available ingredients?

  1. Enter ingredient names or take a photo to identify ingredients.
  2. Select the cooking equipment available to you.
  3. Review recipe ideas and read every step before cooking.

AI-assisted suggestions may need adjustments for serving size, allergies and the actual condition of your ingredients.

Frequently asked questions

Can CookFit suggest recipes from ingredients I already have?

Yes. Enter ingredients manually or use a photo as the starting point for recipe ideas.

Can I select my cooking equipment?

Yes. CookFit supports options such as an air fryer, oven, stovetop or microwave.

Do AI recipes replace food-safety guidance?

No. Check allergens, safe cooking temperatures and food storage before eating.
