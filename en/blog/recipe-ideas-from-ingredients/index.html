Recipe ideas from ingredients you have: how to begin

When you are not sure what to cook, start with usable food and the equipment in your kitchen. A clear ingredient list helps a recipe-idea tool return more relevant suggestions.

MGM Studio›Blog›Recipe ideas from ingredients

1. Check ingredients before searching for a recipe

Discard spoiled food, check use-by dates and separate anything that may trigger an allergy. List the main ingredients, approximate amounts and basic seasonings available.

2. Should you type ingredients or use a photo?

Manual entry works well when you know each item. A fridge photo is a faster starting point, but image recognition needs review. Add or remove anything the initial list gets wrong.

3. Match ideas to your cooking equipment

The same ingredients may suit an oven, air fryer, stovetop or microwave in different ways. Selecting the equipment first reduces suggestions that you cannot prepare.

4. Read the entire recipe before cooking

Review time, portions, temperatures and preparation steps before you begin. AI-assisted suggestions can be incomplete or wrong; they do not replace allergen, doneness or food-safety guidance.

5. Give enough detail for a useful suggestion

  • Name ingredients that need to be used first.
  • Select the equipment and time available.
  • State the number of servings and foods to avoid.
  • Ask for fewer steps if you are a beginner.
